note=The real-Internet tier: the SAME full multi-edge topology as the other tiers (scenarios/five-edge-100.yaml, 103 services) with the cloud collector running on Crane Cloud (reached over HTTP across the public Internet) and the 5-device/100-service edge half running locally. Each edge device's telemetry to the cloud goes out through its own source IP (10.30.0.<device>, NAT'd to the uplink), so a partition is a REAL per-device cut: an iptables DROP of that device's traffic to the Crane host, while the harness keeps polling Crane over the host's own address. The collector RCA loop ticks every 500 ms, so one tick is the time unit; one sim step (100 ms) maps to one real tick (E3). Two WAN-tier settings, both disclosed: the edge services report at 500 ms (not 200 ms) so 100 services do not overload Crane's free-tier ingress over the real link, which makes the edge time-to-diagnosis about two ticks rather than one; and cuts are capped at 40 s because a longer partition over the uncontrolled Internet does not cleanly re-establish all of a device's HTTP streams on heal. With both, the edge collector localises its device's fault locally (100%), the Crane cloud is blind during every cut (cannot localise the edge fault), and it loses exactly the cut device's services (cloud_min_visible = 103 minus the device size), on a genuine Internet path.
