note=The collector RCA loop ticks every 500 ms (tokio interval in crates/mesh/src/main.rs run_collector), so one diagnostic tick is the natural time unit; the simulation STEP_MS is 100 ms, so one sim step maps to one real diagnostic tick (E3 calibration). This tier runs the SAME full multi-edge scenario as the single-node and k3s tiers (scenarios/five-edge-100.yaml: 5 edge devices of 20/15/25/18/22 services + 3 cloud services = 103 services) across real VMs on a single-host Kolla-Ansible OpenStack (Ubuntu 24.04), one VM per edge device plus a cloud VM. The mesh is the static musl binary run natively on cirros guests; the host drives them through the neutron DHCP namespace (no floating IPs); the partition is a genuine in-VM blackhole route to the cloud. Two deploy conditions matter at this scale and are handled by the harness: the VMs start from a clean boot (a redeploy over a running mesh leaves a stale baseline), and the run waits for all 103 services to connect before the per-service baselines freeze (otherwise a late-connecting service never gets a clean baseline). With both, the edge collector localises its device's fault and the cloud is blind during the cut, exactly as on the dedicated tiers.
