note=The collector RCA loop ticks every 500 ms (tokio interval in crates/mesh/src/main.rs run_collector), so one diagnostic tick is the natural time unit; the simulation STEP_MS is 100 ms, so one sim step maps to one real diagnostic tick (E3 calibration). This tier runs the reduced multi-edge scenario (scenarios/openstack-2edge.yaml: 2 edge devices + cloud, 14 services) across real VMs on a single-host Kolla-Ansible OpenStack (Ubuntu 24.04, 14 GiB laptop), one VM per edge device plus a cloud VM. The partition is a genuine in-VM blackhole route to the cloud. The cloud-blind outcome (cloud localises 0/edge-fault during the cut, and loses exactly the cut device's services) is robust. Edge time-to-diagnosis is noise-limited here: on the shared-host VMs, vCPU scheduling jitter disrupts the thin score margin between a culprit and its immediate call-graph ancestor, so the edge collector localises intermittently; the clean edge TtD measurement is on the dedicated single-node and k3s tiers.
